Privacy Policy

What we collect, why we collect it, and how we look after your information.

Business: Final Touch Window Cleaning · Phone: (310) 755-9071 · Email: finaltouchsouthbay@gmail.com

1. Information we collect

We only collect what we need to quote and deliver your service. That typically includes:

  • Your full name
  • Your phone number
  • Your email address, if you email us
  • The city or property address where the work will take place
  • The message or service details you send us

We may also receive basic, non-identifying technical information from your browser, such as device type and pages viewed, which helps us keep the website working properly.

2. How we use your information

  • To respond to your quote request and prepare a complimentary estimate
  • To schedule, perform and follow up on your home exterior cleaning service
  • To contact you by phone, text or email about your request or appointment
  • To keep a record of work performed at your property

We do not sell, rent or trade your personal information.

3. Text messages

If you provide your phone number through a form on this site, you agree to receive text messages from Final Touch Window Cleaning relating to your inquiry and service. Message frequency varies, and message and data rates may apply. You can opt out at any time by replying STOP or by asking us directly. Opting out of texts does not prevent us from calling you about a scheduled appointment.

4. Sharing your information

We share information only where it is necessary to run our business — for example with service providers who help us host this website, manage bookings, or process payments. Those providers are only permitted to use your information to perform services for us. We may also disclose information where required by law.

5. Reviews and third-party links

This website links to our Google Business Profile and social media pages. Anything you post publicly on those platforms — including reviews — is governed by that platform's own privacy policy, not this one.

6. Cookies

This website uses only the minimum cookies or local storage needed for the page to function correctly. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings; some parts of the site may behave differently if you do.

7. Data retention and security

We keep your information for as long as it is needed to provide services and maintain business records, and we take reasonable steps to protect it against loss, misuse and unauthorized access. No method of transmission over the internet is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Your choices

You may ask us at any time to review, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you. Contact finaltouchsouthbay@gmail.com or call (310) 755-9071 and we will act on your request promptly.

9. Children's privacy

Our services are directed to property owners and managers. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The version posted on this page is the current one.

11. Contact us

Questions about your privacy? Call (310) 755-9071 or email finaltouchsouthbay@gmail.com.
